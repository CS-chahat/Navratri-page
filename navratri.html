Celebrating the nine forms of Divine Mother.Click or view each entry to read a short note on the importance of each Devi..!

Shailaputri Day 1

Shailaputri - "Daughter of the Mountain" is worshipped on the first day.She symbolizes strength,purity and the root of spiritual life.

Bramacharani Day 2

Bramacharani - the austere mother who practices devotion and penance.She embodies discipline,spiritual seeking and steadfastness on the path towards inner wisdom.

Chandraghanta Day 3

Chandraghanta - with a half-moon on her forehead.She represents courage,serenity and readiness to battle negative forces while maintaining compassion.

Kushmanda Day 4

Kushmanda is credited with creating the universe with her divine smile.She symbolizes cosmic energy,abundance and the power to dispel darkness and bring light.

Skandamata Day 5

Skandamata - mother of Skanda(Kartikeya).She is worshipped for maternal love and power.

Katyayani Day 6

Katyayani - born to the sage katyayan-is a fierce form of Durga who defeats demons.She symbolizes righteous action and bravery.

Kalaratri Day 7

Kalaratri - the darkest, most fearsome form- who destroys ignorance.Worship of Kalaratri grants strenght to overcome fear and transforms destructive energies into protection.

Mahagauri Day 8

Mahagauri - the radiant white goddess representing purity,peace and forgiveness.She blesses devotees with serenity,beauty and the removal of blemishes or suffering.

Siddhidatri Day 9

Siddhidatri - the granter of supernatural powers(siddhis).On the final day she symbolizes perfection,spiritual accomplishment and the fulfillment of devotees' highest aspiration.
